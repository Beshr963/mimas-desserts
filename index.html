Our Story

Mimas Desserts was born from a passion for authentic Middle Eastern sweets. Our family recipes have been perfected over generations, bringing the most exquisite flavours from the heart of the Middle East to Eastbourne.

Every piece of Baklava, every slice of Kunafa is handcrafted with love using only the finest ingredients. Come visit us at 108 Cavendish Place, Eastbourne.
