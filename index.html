Mimas Desserts was born from a passion for authentic Middle Eastern sweets. Our family recipes have been perfected over generations, bringing you the most exquisite flavors from the heart of the Middle East.

Every piece of baklava, every slice of kunafa is handcrafted with love using only the finest ingredients – premium pistachios, pure honey, and the freshest dairy.
